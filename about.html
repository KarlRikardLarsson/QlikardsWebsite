Hi, I'm Rikard.

Business Intelligence consultant at Kolibri Vision in Stockholm, Qlik enthusiast all the time. Most people online know me as Qlikard — a name that kind of chose itself.

Truth be told, this site is mostly for me. It's the notebook I keep coming back to — the patterns I'd otherwise re-derive, the benchmarks I'd forget, the scripts I'd dig out of a three-year-old project to find again. Writing things down once means solving them once. If it helps you skip a trawl through some forum thread along the way — even better.

What you'll find here

  • Scripts — Load script patterns, data modeling, QVD best practices
  • Visualizations — Chart design, set analysis, expressions that actually work
  • Qlik Automate — Step-by-step automation flows and integrations
  • General — Everything else that makes Qlik a little less painful

Who this is for

Whether you're a developer deep in the load script, an analyst trying to get a chart to behave, or someone who just got handed a Qlik project — there's something here for you.
